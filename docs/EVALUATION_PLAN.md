# ReviveAI — Evaluation Plan

## Razorpay Buildathon — Track 03: AI Revenue Recovery

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Evaluation Objective

The purpose of this evaluation plan is to prove that ReviveAI can:

1. Correctly identify revenue at risk.
2. Estimate the probability of recovery.
3. Diagnose payment/revenue-loss situations.
4. Recommend appropriate recovery actions.
5. Respect deterministic financial guardrails.
6. Execute permitted recovery actions.
7. Verify successful recovery.
8. Measure actual revenue recovered.
9. Fail safely when uncertainty or infrastructure failures occur.
10. Maintain a complete audit trail.

The system must be evaluated on:

- Machine Learning performance
- AI Agent performance
- Recovery effectiveness
- Safety
- Reliability
- Business impact
